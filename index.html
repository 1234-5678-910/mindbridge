Background

Research Context

We surveyed 127 Mahindra University students (18–25 yrs) and conducted 5 in-depth interviews to understand how young adults navigate unexplained experiences and beliefs in black magic. Many sit in a "gray zone" between scientific education and cultural tradition, often facing distress without integrated support.

Our research uncovered a critical gap: existing support systems — medical, spiritual, or digital — fail to honour the complexity of this lived experience. Students wanted empathy, not judgment; understanding, not dismissal.

"I don't know what I believe anymore. It sits in this uncomfortable middle space."

— Interview Participant (Pseudonym: Akhil)

User Research

The Human Challenge

Pain Points

  • Cognitive Dissonance

    Tension between scientific education and lived spiritual experience

  • Fragmented Support

    Medical dismisses spirituality; spiritual ignores mental health

  • Social Isolation

    Fear of judgment prevents people from seeking help

  • Exploitation Risk

    Unverified practitioners exploit vulnerable individuals

  • No Safe Space

    No platform for processing uncertainty without stigma

Validated Needs

  • Integrated Support Pathways

    Medical + cultural support working together, not in silos

  • Practitioner Verification

    System to identify legitimate vs exploitative practitioners

  • Accessible Psychoeducation

    Respectful, jargon-free information about beliefs and psychology

  • Anonymous Spaces

    Judgment-free discussion without identity exposure

  • Immediate Grounding Tools

    Rapid distress relief during acute episodes

5 Whys Analysis

Digging to the Core

Problem Statement

Young adults experiencing supernatural distress have no safe, effective support

Why #1

Existing support systems (medical or spiritual) do not integrate both dimensions

Why #2

Cultural beliefs are treated as invalid by medical institutions and vice versa

Why #3

No shared framework exists to hold scientific and cultural explanations simultaneously

Why #4

Technology solutions in this space have been built for one audience, not the "gray zone"

🎯 Root Cause

No culturally-sensitive platforms exist that (1) distinguish legitimate practices from exploitation, (2) explain psychological mechanisms accessibly, (3) provide integrated support pathways, and (4) create safe spaces for the "gray zone" of uncertainty.

Selected Solution

Introducing MindBridge

A Prompt-Powered Supernatural Distress Hotline

MindBridge is a culturally-sensitive AI chatbot built entirely through careful system prompting and free LLM APIs. It meets users in moments of distress with empathy first, judgment never. No model training or complex backend required.

Belief-Aware Responses

Validates spiritual views, gently offers psychological insights without dismissal

Immediate Grounding

One-tap breathing exercises & 5-4-3-2-1 technique for acute distress

Dual-Perspective Education

Explains sleep paralysis, nocebo effect alongside cultural meanings

Verified Resource Directory

Campus counselors + community-vetted practitioners in one place

Crisis Escalation

Hard-coded rules for self-harm language → emergency contacts + human handoff

Privacy-First

No login, no data storage unless explicitly opted in by the user
